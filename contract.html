PMBA Airlines Team Contract

The norms, expectations, and accountability agreements the team committed to for MGT 6050 and the marketing simulation.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Purpose

We will work constructively as a team to achieve positive outcomes on all tasks. Our goals are to consistently place among the top three teams during competition-style activities and to support one another in maintaining a GPA of 3.0 or higher.

We will collaborate as efficiently as possible while helping each other grow as students and professionals.

Team scorecard (Google Sheet)  ·  Original interactive role site

2. Norms

Be present and prepared.

We will arrive on time to all meetings and classes. If a team member is unable to attend a meeting, they will review the meeting minutes for action items and key discussion points and reach out to the group with any questions.

Communicate consistently.

Text will be our preferred method of communication, with email as a secondary option. We will communicate proactively if we need assistance with a task or assignment or are unable to attend a meeting.

Coordinate meeting times.

We will prioritize meeting after class on Tuesdays. When another meeting time is needed, we will coordinate through the group text to find a time that works for everyone.

Contribute equally.

Each team member will complete their assigned work on time and contribute their fair share to the team.

3. Expectations

Assume positive intent.

We will approach one another with positivity and respect and address unintended impact openly and constructively.

Create space for every voice.

We will listen without interrupting and ensure everyone has an equal opportunity to contribute.

Make it safe to say, “I don’t know.”

We will encourage questions, requests for help, and honest acknowledgement of uncertainty or mistakes.

Respect different working styles and circumstances.

We will communicate our needs clearly, avoid assumptions, and work together to find solutions.

Anyone can call for a pause or reset.

Any team member may pause an unproductive or uncomfortable conversation so the group can clarify the issue, reset, and move forward constructively.

4. Responsibilities & Division of Labor

We will rotate team roles and responsibilities each week. If a team member believes they would be better suited for a different role on a particular assignment or project, team members may trade roles when doing so will help the group produce its best work.

The Deviant will serve as an additional rotating role and will be randomly assigned every Tuesday evening before class.

5. Decision-Making & Coordination

  • We will discuss significant decisions as a team and work toward a group consensus. If a final decision cannot be reached through discussion, the Team Leader for the week will make the final decision.
  • At the beginning of each team meeting, the Team Leader will review upcoming due dates, priorities, and assigned tasks to ensure that work is not duplicated or missed.
  • We will meet in person if we can. However, we can meet through other means such as Zoom. If we need to communicate quicker, then we can communicate via text or email.
  • If we have a deadline for an assignment (it’s urgent), then the team will turn to a vote on an assignment.
  • If a team member drops the program, then we will meet as a team, evaluate the roles and contract as a whole, and make decisions on what to update as a group.

6. Accountability & Conflict Resolution

When challenges or conflicts arise, we will use the following process:

Address issues early.

Discuss concerns promptly before they develop into larger team problems.

Listen without interrupting.

Give each team member an opportunity to share their perspective.

Identify common goals and brainstorm solutions.

Identify what the team is trying to accomplish, generate multiple potential solutions, and encourage input from the entire group.

Select a solution and create an action plan.

Agree on the best path forward, document the action plan, and schedule a follow-up when appropriate to ensure the solution is working for everyone.

Failure to adhere to the agreements outlined in this contract will result in the following:

  • First violation: The Team Leader for the week will issue a warning.
  • Second violation: The team member will bring a treat for the rest of the group to Tuesday’s class.
  • Third violation: The team member will wear a wizard hat for the entirety of Tuesday’s class.
